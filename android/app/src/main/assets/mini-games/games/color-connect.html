How to Play

Goal

Connect each pair of matching colored dots with a continuous path. Fill every cell in the grid — no empty cells allowed!

Controls

  • Click/tap and drag from any dot or existing path cell to draw
  • Drag back over your own path to shorten it
  • Drawing over another color erases that color's path from that point onward
  • Only horizontal and vertical moves — no diagonals

Winning

  • All color pairs must be connected
  • Every cell in the grid must be covered
  • No paths may overlap or cross

Hints

  • Tap 💡 Hint to reveal the correct path for the color with least progress
  • You start each puzzle with 10 hints

Difficulty

  • Easy (4×4) — 3 color pairs, great for beginners
  • Medium (5×5) — 4–5 color pairs
  • Hard (7×7) — 5–6 color pairs
FAQ

Is every puzzle solvable?

Yes — all levels are hand-crafted and verified to have a valid solution that fills the entire grid.

Is my best score saved?

Yes — your best move count is saved per difficulty in your browser's local storage.

What happens if I draw over another color?

That color's path is erased from the point you drew over. You can then connect it again differently.

Why can't I draw diagonally?

Paths only travel horizontally or vertically — this is the core rule of Color Connect puzzles.
