How to Play

Goal

Slide all tiles in one direction. Tiles with the same number merge into one tile with their sum. Reach the 2048 tile to win!

Controls

  • Arrow keys — slide all tiles in that direction (desktop)
  • Swipe — swipe left/right/up/down on the board (mobile)

Strategy Tips

  • Keep your highest tile in a corner
  • Build a chain of decreasing values towards that corner
  • Avoid filling the board — plan ahead!
FAQ

What happens after reaching 2048?

You win, but the game continues! You can keep merging tiles to reach 4096, 8192, and beyond.

Is my best score saved?

Yes — your best score is automatically saved in your browser's local storage.

Can I undo a move?

Not currently. Think carefully before each swipe!
