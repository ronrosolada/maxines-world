How to Play

Goal

Whack as many moles as possible before time runs out or you lose all 3 lives!

Controls

  • Desktop: Click on a mole when it pops up
  • Mobile: Tap on a mole when it pops up

Scoring & Levels

  • +1 point for each mole whacked
  • Lose a life when a mole disappears unwhacked
  • Game ends when all 3 lives are lost — no time limit!
  • Level 2 at score 8 — moles speed up
  • Level 3 at score 18 — two moles appear simultaneously
  • Level 4 at score 32 — three moles at once, very fast
FAQ

Is my best score saved?

Yes — your best score is saved in your browser's local storage and persists between sessions.

Why do moles appear faster over time?

Each level brings faster moles and more of them. Levels unlock at scores 8, 18, and 32 — level 3 and 4 send two or three moles popping at once!

Can I play on mobile?

Yes! Just tap the moles when they appear. The grid scales to fit your screen.

Is there a time limit?

No! You play as long as you can keep your lives. Miss a mole and lose a ❤️ — lose all 3 and it's game over.
