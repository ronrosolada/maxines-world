How to Play

Goal

Place colorful block shapes onto the 10×10 grid. Fill an entire row or column to clear it and score bonus points. Keep placing pieces as long as they fit — the game ends when none of your 3 current pieces can fit anywhere.

Controls

  • Drag a piece directly from the shelf onto the grid to place it
  • A shadow preview shows where the piece will land — green tint = valid, red = invalid

Scoring

  • Each cell you fill: +1 point
  • Clearing a full row: +10 bonus points
  • Clearing a full column: +10 bonus points
  • Multiple rows/columns cleared at once each give +10
FAQ

How do I earn bonus points?

Fill every cell in a row or column. When a line is completely filled it clears automatically and you earn +10 bonus points per line cleared.

When do I get new pieces?

You always have 3 pieces on the shelf. Once all 3 have been placed, a fresh set of 3 random pieces appears. You can place them in any order.

What counts as game over?

The game ends when none of the 3 pieces currently on your shelf can fit anywhere on the grid.

Is my best score saved?

Yes — your best score is automatically saved in your browser's local storage and persists between sessions.
