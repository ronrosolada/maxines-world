How to Play

Goal

Move all 52 cards to the 4 foundation piles in the top-right, building each suit from Ace up to King (A → 2 → … → K).

Layout

  • Free Cells (top-left): 4 temporary holding spots — one card each
  • Foundations (top-right): build up by suit from Ace to King
  • Tableau (bottom): 8 columns of face-up cards

Moving Cards

  • Click a card (or the start of a valid sequence) to select it — it highlights blue
  • Click a valid destination to move: green highlights show legal targets
  • Click the same card again to deselect
  • In the tableau, stack cards in descending rank and alternating color (red on black, black on red)
  • Sequences: you can move a sequence of correctly ordered cards at once, as long as you have enough free cells and empty columns

Controls

  • Desktop & Mobile: tap/click to select and move cards
  • Undo: step back through your moves (unlimited)
  • New Game: deal a fresh shuffled deck

Winning

All four foundation piles complete (Ace through King for each suit). Try to do it in as few moves as possible!

FAQ

How many cards can I move at once?

The maximum sequence length you can move is (empty free cells + 1) × 2empty columns. With 4 empty free cells and 2 empty columns you could move up to 20 cards at once.

Can every deal be won?

Nearly all FreeCell deals are solvable — only about 1 in 1000 random deals is unsolvable. If you get stuck, use the Undo button to backtrack and try a different path.

Is my best score saved?

Yes — your lowest move count for a winning game is saved automatically in your browser's local storage and persists between sessions.

Why can't I move this sequence?

You don't have enough free cells and empty tableau columns. Move some cards to free cells first, or clear a tableau column to unlock more capacity.

Can I move a card off the foundation?

No — once a card reaches a foundation pile it stays there. Plan your foundation moves carefully.
