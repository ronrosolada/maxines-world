How to Play

Goal

Guide the bird through as many pipe gaps as possible without crashing.

Controls

  • Desktop: Press Space or click the canvas to flap
  • Mobile: Tap anywhere on the canvas to flap

Levels & Time of Day

  • Start at night — stars and a crescent moon light your way
  • Score 5: dawn breaks — orange sky, rising sun
  • Score 10: full day — blue sky with drifting clouds
  • Score 20: dusk — red evening sky, setting sun
  • Each level also increases pipe speed and narrows the gap

Distance

Your total flight distance is tracked in metres (km once you're flying far!).

FAQ

Is my best score saved?

Yes — your best score is saved in your browser's local storage and persists between sessions.

Why does the game get harder?

Pipes speed up and gaps narrow as your score increases, keeping the challenge alive through 4 levels.

What kills the bird?

Hitting a pipe (top or bottom), touching the ground, or flying into the ceiling all end the game.

Can I play on mobile?

Yes! Just tap anywhere on the game canvas to make the bird flap.
