How to Play

Goal

Match tiles to score points. Once you've scored enough, new tiles stop dropping — clear the remaining tiles to advance to the next level.

Controls — Desktop

  • Click a tile to select it (glows white), then click an adjacent tile to swap
  • Clicking a non-adjacent tile re-selects it as your new choice
  • Only swaps that create a match are allowed

Controls — Mobile

  • Tap to select, tap adjacent to swap — or swipe directly from one tile to a neighbor

Bomb

Press 💣 Bomb, then click any tile to blast a 5×5 area. You start with 3 and earn +1 per level (max 3).

Hint

Press 💡 Hint to see a valid swap highlighted in gold — the tiles slowly animate to show the move. You start with 3 hints and earn +1 per level (max 3).

Scoring

  • 3 tiles → 30 pts · 4 tiles → 60 pts · 5+ tiles → 150 pts
  • Each cascade step doubles the multiplier

Level Progression

Each level is harder and gives you fewer moves (min 12). Clear the board to advance.

FAQ

Why did tiles stop dropping from the top?

You've scored enough — match the remaining tiles until the board is empty to advance to the next level.

Why can't I swap these two tiles?

Only swaps that immediately create a match of 3 or more are allowed. Invalid swaps animate back.

What if there are no valid moves left during clearing?

The board shuffles the remaining tiles so you can always keep playing.

Is my best score saved?

Yes — saved in your browser's local storage and persists across sessions.
