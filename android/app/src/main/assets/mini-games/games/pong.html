How to Play

Goal

Score 7 points before your opponent by getting the ball past their paddle.

Controls

  • Your paddle: W / S or ↑ / ↓
  • Mobile: drag on your chosen side of the court
  • PvP — P1 (left): W / S  ·  P2 (right): ↑ / ↓

Modes & Side

  • vs AI — play the computer; choose ◀ Left or Right ▶ before starting
  • vs Player — two players share the same keyboard or screen
  • Mode and side can be changed between games but not mid-rally

Ball Speed

The ball speeds up slightly with each paddle hit, making rallies progressively more intense.

FAQ

Is my best score saved?

Yes — total wins vs AI are saved in your browser's local storage and persist between sessions.

How does the AI difficulty work?

The AI has a slight reaction delay and imperfect tracking, so it's beatable with sharp angle shots to the corners.

Can I play on mobile?

Yes! Drag your finger on the left half of the court to control your paddle. In PvP mode, a second player can use the right half simultaneously.

What affects the angle of the ball?

Where the ball contacts the paddle matters — hitting the edge sends it at a steep angle, hitting the centre returns it flatter.
