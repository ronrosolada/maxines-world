How to Play

Goal

Tap colored tiles as they fall in time with the music. You have 3 lives — each tile that slips past the bottom costs one life. Game over when all lives are gone.

Controls

Click or tap on the colored tiles as they scroll down the screen. Tapping empty space does nothing — only tiles count!

Difficulty & Songs

Easy songs fall slowly (~65–74 BPM). Medium falls at a moderate pace (~96–112 BPM). Hard songs fall fast (140–160 BPM). Each difficulty has 5 songs — pick one or let the demo play.

Lives

You start with 3 ❤️ lives. A tile passing the bottom without being tapped removes one life. Tapping empty space is forgiving — no penalty!

Scoring & Levels

Each tapped tile scores 10 points. Speed increases every 10 tiles. Best score is saved separately per difficulty.

FAQ

What happens when I tap empty space?

Nothing — tapping empty space is harmless. Only tiles that pass the bottom without being tapped cost a life.

Is my best score saved?

Yes — best scores are saved per difficulty (Easy / Medium / Hard) in browser local storage.

Can I pick a specific song?

Yes! The welcome screen lets you choose difficulty first, then pick any song from that difficulty's pool. There's also a Random option.
