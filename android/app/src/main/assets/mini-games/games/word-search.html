How to Play

Goal

Find all the hidden words in the grid before time runs out.

Controls

  • Click and drag from the first letter to the last letter of a word
  • Touch and drag works the same on mobile
  • Words can go in any direction on Medium and Hard

Scoring

  • 100 points per word found
  • Bonus: 2 × remaining seconds on a clean win

Timer

  • Easy — 3 minutes
  • Medium — 2 min 30 sec
  • Hard — 2 minutes
FAQ

Can words overlap?

Yes — letters can be shared between words.

Do I need to drag in the exact direction?

Just drag from the first letter to the last. Both forward and backward directions are accepted.

Is my best score saved?

Yes, your best score per difficulty is saved in your browser's local storage.

What themes appear?

Each game picks a random theme from Animals, Colors, or Sports.
