How to Play

Goal

Fill all 13 scoring categories over 13 rounds to maximize your total score.

Each Turn

  • Roll 5 dice up to 3 times per turn
  • Click a die to hold it between rolls — it turns gold and lifts up
  • After rolling, click a category in the scorecard to score it
  • Grey numbers show what each unfilled category would score with your current dice

Upper Section

  • Ones–Sixes: Sum of all dice showing that number
  • Upper Bonus: Score ≥63 in the upper section to earn +35 bonus points

Lower Section

  • Three/Four of a Kind: Sum of all dice (if condition met)
  • Full House: Three of one + two of another = 25 pts
  • Small Straight: 4 sequential dice = 30 pts
  • Large Straight: 5 sequential dice = 40 pts
  • Yahtzee: All 5 dice the same = 50 pts; extra Yahtzees score +100 bonus each
  • Chance: Sum of all 5 dice, no conditions
FAQ

What if I can't score anything useful?

You must still choose a category every turn — click any unfilled category to score it (even for 0 points). Every category must be filled exactly once.

Can I re-use a category?

No — each of the 13 categories can only be scored once per game.

What is the upper bonus?

If your total score in the Ones through Sixes categories is 63 or more, you earn a +35 bonus. To hit 63 you generally need to score three of each number (e.g. three 3s = 9, three 6s = 18, etc.).

What happens with extra Yahtzees?

If you roll a Yahtzee after already scoring 50 pts in the Yahtzee category, you earn a +100 bonus and must score the roll in another open category.

Is my best score saved?

Yes — your highest score is saved in your browser's local storage and persists between sessions.
