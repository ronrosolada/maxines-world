### 1. Playroom Home — Too many equally weighted cards flatten the visual hierarchy
- **Current look & feel:** The 16dp rhythm, repeated white/cream surfaces, and similar 12–14dp corner radii make Today Quest, streak, Arena, Quick Bits, subjects, stickers, and sanctuary compete at nearly the same visual volume. Against the saturated GoldTop → GoldMid → Coral gradient, the long page risks feeling like a stack of floating panels rather than one inviting playroom.
- **Design fix (concrete):** Create three visual tiers. Keep **Today Quest** as the only elevated hero card (`RoundedCornerShape(20.dp)`, 20–24dp internal padding, subtle Ink-tinted shadow); render streak and Quick Bits as lower-emphasis cream tonal cards with little or no elevation; reserve white cards for primary actionable content such as Arena and subject cards. On tablets, use 24dp page gutters, 20–24dp between major sections, and 12dp within card groups; soften the gradient behind scrolling content with a translucent Cream wash—approximately `Cream.copy(alpha = .16f)`—so the lower Coral area does not overpower cards. Use Baloo 2 only for the page greeting and section headings, while balances, chips, and metadata remain Nunito.
- **Why it improves the feel:** Maxine immediately sees where to begin instead of scanning a wall of equally cheerful choices. The page remains warm and playful, but feels intentionally composed rather than visually busy.

### 2. Today Quest Card — The quest’s core action is buried inside several decorative signals
- **Current look & feel:** The paw header, circular Milo portrait, task copy, paw progress, count label, and reward strip all demand attention inside one white card. Gold appears both as progress and reward emphasis, so the eye does not get a clear sequence from “what to do” to “how close I am” to “what I earn.”
- **Design fix (concrete):** Build a strict three-part hierarchy: a compact header row, a task row, and a progress/reward footer. Use a 56dp Milo circle rather than 64dp, set the task in Nunito Bold 18/24 with Ink, place “X of Y” directly beside the progress row, and keep 12dp between these elements; use `Arrangement.spacedBy(12.dp)` rather than distributing empty space. Make filled paws `VillageTeal` and reserve `SunshineGold` for the reward strip, using `SunshineGold.copy(alpha = .22f)` with `OnGold`; increase unfilled paws to Ink at roughly 28% alpha for clearer visibility. Animate only the newly completed paw with a 180–220ms scale-and-settle motion, while the reward strip uses a restrained color fade instead of another pop.
- **Why it improves the feel:** The card reads as a tiny story—task, progress, reward—without losing Milo’s warmth. Separating teal progress from gold reward also makes completion easier to understand at a glance.

### 3. Assessment Arena — Filters, pack cards, and celebration styling risk becoming one continuous layer of spectacle
- **Current look & feel:** Subject-colored chips, star-heavy pack cards, backdrops, gradients, trophies, and result celebrations all carry strong visual personality. When these treatments appear together, the Arena can feel louder than the rest of the app and make pack titles or completion states harder to scan.
- **Design fix (concrete):** Keep the browsing state visually disciplined: unselected filter chips should use Cream or the subject’s pale tinted surface with Ink text and a 1dp subject-accent outline; only the selected chip should use the solid subject accent with its verified on-color. Give pack cards a consistent `RoundedCornerShape(16.dp)`, 16dp padding, pale subject surface, and one accent edge or icon container rather than full-card saturated gradients; place star ratings in a stable footer row with 8dp spacing. Reserve gradients, trophy scale-up, and confetti-like motion exclusively for the result state, using one 350–450ms celebratory sequence followed by a still composition; honor reduced motion with opacity and color transitions only.
- **Why it improves the feel:** Browsing feels focused and game-like rather than noisy, while success screens retain a genuine sense of occasion. Maxine can distinguish “choose a pack” from “I won” through visual intensity alone.

### 4. Wildlife Stickers and Sanctuary — Locked and earned rewards need a clearer material contrast
- **Current look & feel:** Gold/cream gradients, muted beige slots, dashed borders, sticker art, and sanctuary progress create an appealing keepsake theme, but the two preview sections may read as variations of the same card. Dashed locked slots can also become visually scratchy when many appear together, especially beside detailed animal illustrations.
- **Design fix (concrete):** Establish one “keepsake board” container language: Cream surface, 20dp corners, 20dp padding, and a subtle warm border such as `SunshineGold.copy(alpha = .28f)`. Earned sticker slots should be White with a restrained radial or vertical gold tint, a crisp animal silhouette, and a tiny 2dp inner highlight; locked slots should use Ink at 6–8% for the fill, Ink at 18–22% for a solid 1dp border, and reduced icon opacity instead of repeated dashed outlines. Differentiate the two previews through layout rather than extra decoration: a regular slot grid for stickers and a wider illustrated board treatment for sanctuary progress, with titles in Baloo 2 22/28 and supporting labels in Nunito 14/20.
- **Why it improves the feel:** Earned animals feel precious and tactile, while locked spaces remain calm instead of looking unavailable or unfinished. The collection area gains a coherent scrapbook identity without overwhelming the animal artwork.

### 5. Video Library — The utilitarian list styling feels disconnected from the warmer home experience
- **Current look & feel:** A standard TopAppBar, thumbnail-like rows, play circles, checkmarks, and linear progress indicators provide clarity but can resemble a generic media list. Switching to a broad Surface on tablets may further flatten the page and leave lesson rows floating in excess white space.
- **Design fix (concrete):** Use a Cream page background with a centered tablet content rail of approximately 840–960dp (`widthIn(max = 920.dp)`), 24dp horizontal padding, and 12dp spacing between lesson cards. Give cards 16dp corners, 14–16dp internal padding, a pale subject-tinted thumbnail well, and a single subject-colored play button; keep titles at Nunito Bold 18/24 and metadata at 14/20. Style progress tracks with Ink at 10% and the active bar with the relevant subject accent, using 6dp height and rounded caps; show completion as a small `SuccessGreen` badge rather than allowing checkmarks to compete with the play control.
- **Why it improves the feel:** The library stays calm and highly readable while still belonging to Maxine’s warm, illustrated world. On tablets, the constrained rail produces a deliberate reading rhythm rather than a stretched desktop-like list.

**Highest-impact single change:** Turn the Playroom Home into a clear three-tier hierarchy, with Today Quest as the sole hero and all supporting cards visually quieter.
