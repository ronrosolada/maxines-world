## Variant: Clean Studio

### Design stance
Material-3 calm — airy cards, restrained palette, progress first. The "learning dashboard" reading of the app: what to do today, how the week is going, what's unlocked.

### Key choices
- Layout: 2-column subject card grid + sticky right rail (Today's Quest, This Week ring, Mini-games)
- Typography: Baloo 2 for headings, Nunito body — tighter sizes, more whitespace
- Color: neutral surface (#F7F5EF) with white cards; subjects reduced to icon chips + accent bars
- Interaction: tasks check off; subject cards open lesson modal; mini-game rows hover

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: scannability, parent visibility of progress, calm low-stimulus UI
- Weak at: least "magical" of the three — the village character is toned down
- Note: subject grid leaves a gap vs the taller sidebar on tall viewports (accept for now, or balance with a featured-lesson card)

### Best for
- Parents who want progress clarity and a calmer screen; a good "main dashboard" candidate
