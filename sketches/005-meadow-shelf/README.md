## Variant: Meadow Shelf

### Design stance
The Khan Academy Kids treatment: soft pastels, a wooden bookshelf of today's books, animal guides, calm and generous. Learning is framed as reading.

### Key choices
- Layout: guide band (Learn/Read/ABC/Play tabs) → wooden shelf with 6 book cards → friends row + quest card
- Typography: Baloo 2 display, Nunito body — slightly smaller, airier
- Color: pastel surfaces per subject (soft purple/blue/pink/green), brown shelf anchor
- Interaction: books open lesson modal; quest checks off; tabs switch active state; friend cards hover

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: calm, low-stimulus, parent-friendly progress framing; bookshelf reads instantly
- Weak at: least gamified of the three — streak/XP are reduced to plain pills
- Watch out: 6 books across a 3:2 landscape works; a longer shelf needs horizontal scroll or pagination

### Best for
- Maxine's reading-forward personality; a gentle everyday-home screen
