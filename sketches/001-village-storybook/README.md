## Variant: Village Storybook

### Design stance
The app as a warm picture book — cream canvas, storybook cottages, a sun-and-hills hero. Calm, cozy, character-led.

### Key choices
- Layout: 3-column cottage grid (2 rows) + right parchment Daily Quest card; bottom pill nav
- Typography: Baloo 2 display (bounce, rounded), Nunito body — the app's real fonts
- Color: full core palette on Cream (#FFF7E8); each subject = its own cottage accent color
- Interaction: click a cottage → lesson-list modal; Daily Quest tasks toggle done/undone

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: charm, brand warmth, subject color-coding, gentle feel for a young child
- Weak at: lesson progress is secondary (small bars); less "game" energy

### Best for
- Keeping the current friendly village identity but making it calmer and more readable on the tablet
