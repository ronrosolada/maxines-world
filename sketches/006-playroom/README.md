## Variant: Playroom

### Design stance
The ABCmouse/Lingokids treatment: warm orange room, oversized activity islands, sticker book, paw-print progress. Play is the frame, learning hides inside.

### Key choices
- Layout: 3×2 activity islands (Story Time, Number Fun, Kwentuhan, Discovery, Heritage, Kindness) → sticker strip → quest bar
- Typography: Baloo 2 display, Nunito body, big and bouncy
- Color: saturated gold→coral gradient canvas, white islands, subject accent buttons
- Interaction: islands open activity modal; stickers tilt on hover; paw-print dots toggle; locked Kindness island is desaturated with a 🔒 tag

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: immediate "I want to tap that" energy; sticker collection gives a long-term reward loop
- Weak at: busiest of the three — too much for a very calm child; subject hierarchy is weakest
- Watch out: orange canvas needs high-contrast text; keep stickers ≤ 3 rows on tablet

### Best for
- Play-first kids; pairs naturally with the mini-games (Cat Café Dash, Pawprint Parkour)
